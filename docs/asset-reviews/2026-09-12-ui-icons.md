# UI Icon Batch Review — 2026-09-12

**Decision: accepted.** The final 16-icon batch was opened as `ui-icons-contact-sheet.png` and reviewed on both light and dark checkerboard cells at 2× nearest-neighbor scale.

## Generation contract

- Blender: pinned offline 4.2.23 LTS pipeline, EEVEE, transparent RGBA8.
- Frame: fixed item camera, 96×96 pixels, alpha-dilated `#142126` outline.
- Geometry: procedural low-poly primitives only; all icons remain below the 600-triangle hard limit.
- Palette: locked green, leaf, gold, parchment, wood, cyan, crimson, and stone materials.
- Runtime: icons are loaded lazily from `generated/icons/ui_<key>.png` and used by the HUD, main menu, pause/inventory, settings, level-up, shop, and game-over overlays.
- No photo textures, external art, baked rarity glow, clipping, empty frames, or manual paint-over.

The first preview showed the Speed and Continue arrows too small at phone scale. Those two icons were enlarged and rerendered before acceptance. The final contact-sheet SHA-256 is `4b2e50b770db598684e898b50101e243044a8c2a1db123551b4eec56e6d6f8e9`.
