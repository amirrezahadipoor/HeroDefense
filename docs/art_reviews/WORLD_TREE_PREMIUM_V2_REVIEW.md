## Review basis

This is a complete category review rather than a representative spot check. The exact two-state CI artifact was compared with the committed baseline, all **22** runtime frames were decoded and audited, and all six generated review sheets were opened and inspected: the shared state lineup, healthy full-motion sheet, wounded/destruction full-motion sheet, silhouette/material/contrast sheet, complete destruction timeline, and 720×1280 reference-scale Hero composition.

The accepted asset remains an authored low-poly landmark rather than a noisy realistic tree. Its dominant read is a protected living sanctuary: radial buttress roots, a carved cyan heart aperture, paired guardian boughs, and a broad tiered crown. Layered heartwood plates, moss runs, leaf value groups, cut wood, and restrained runes explain material and damage without covering the Hero or combat lanes. Healthy emerald/cyan values transition coherently into charred wood, sparse olive foliage, exposed wounds, and a dimming heart in the wounded state.

All geometry, materials, rigging, motion, and final pixels are generated by the repository's offline Blender/Python pipeline. No external model, texture, stock image, or generated-image payload was introduced.

### Healthy Heartwood Sanctum

The old tapered trunk and foliage blobs were rebuilt as a grounded sanctuary with interlocking trunk masses, thirty broad bark plates, nine buttress roots and knuckles, articulated forked boughs, a carved heart cradle, controlled vine runs, layered canopy clusters, and perimeter leaf rhythm. Emerald value groups stay broad enough for phone scale. The heart and crown breathe asynchronously across the six-frame loop; the root contact remains planted and the first/last pose matches.

### Wounded continuity

The wounded model preserves the same root, aperture, bough, and crown landmarks, so it reads as damage to the defended Tree rather than an unrelated replacement. Its palette is restrained and clearly weaker: charred heartwood, a broken right bough, sparse wilted foliage, four directional wound runes, detached bark, and a reduced cyan core. It remains legible in grayscale and against both tested arena values.

### Destruction presentation

The ten-frame one-shot starts pixel-continuously from wounded Idle, then stages shudder, recoil, cyan core flare, break, fold, fall, impact, and a two-frame final hold. The segmented trunk and crown hinge laterally instead of spinning axially; a few dedicated debris pieces fall without particle clutter. All frames remain safely inside the atlas, the final silhouette is visibly lower and leaning, and the heart contracts rather than popping away.

Runtime selection now shows Healthy above 40% Tree health, Wounded at or below 40%, and Destroying/Destroyed when the Tree or Hero falls. The destruction controller advances on capped presentation time after gameplay simulation has stopped, never loops, and holds frame 9. On defeat, the opaque Game Over summary waits **0.82 seconds**—long enough for the 12 fps destruction to reach its hold—then fades in over **0.28 seconds**; restart touch is disabled until the control is visible. Victory remains immediate because the Tree is standing.
