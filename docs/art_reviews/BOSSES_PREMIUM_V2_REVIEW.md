## Review basis

This is the complete Boss category review, not a representative spot check. The exact four-asset CI artifact was compared with the previously committed runtime catalog. All 112 exported frames were decoded and audited, and all nine generated review sheets were opened and inspected: one shared-scale lineup, four native-size full-motion sheets, and four silhouette/material/readability sheets.

The review sheets use the actual runtime PNG pixels without painted cleanup. They expose transparency over checkerboards, preserve native pixels with nearest-neighbor presentation, and include grayscale, silhouette-only, dark-arena, light-arena, signature-impact, and hit-recoil views. Each Boss now has a separate silhouette, material story, apparent mass, and profile-dispatched signature: ground slam, thorn cage, flame sweep, or void charge. Socket-scale accents are confined to the signature or restrained idle pulses, so readability is raised without persistent visual clutter.

All art in this category is generated by the repository's offline Blender/Python pipeline. No external texture, model, or generated-image payload was introduced.

### Ancient Golem

The pilot monolith was rebuilt as a denser heartstone colossus: interlocking basalt strata, protected cyan core, trilithon crown, boulder joints, split feet, restrained moss, and two unmistakable slam fists. Its heart socket now surges through anticipation and impact. The final bilateral ground slam stays frontal and planted, while the compensated heavy collapse retains every extremity inside the frame.

### Thorn Matriarch

The placeholder cone is now a briar sovereign with a rooted royal gown, layered emerald panels, flower-mask face, ten-petal crown, pollen focus, radial roots, and paired thorn fans. The fans are isolated on hand sockets so the thorn-cage motion visibly contracts, erupts, and settles without covering the face or adding persistent screen noise.

### Ember Wyrm

The former robe-like shape is now a recognizable furnace wyrm with broad faceted wings, horned long muzzle, grasping claws, plated gold belly, digitigrade feet, and a barbed lateral tail. A reserved head-child socket keeps the layered breath hidden outside Attack, then grows it through the left-to-right flame sweep; folded-wing recoil and a safely framed collapse retain the dragon silhouette.

### Void Knight

The former hood placeholder is now a complete abyss champion with layered gunmetal plate, silver edges, asymmetric pauldrons, enclosed helm, connected forked horns, split cape, segmented greaves, and a socket-driven greatblade. A restrained chest-core pulse reinforces the compress-and-launch void charge, while the blade, cape, and armour remain separated in dark and light arena values.
