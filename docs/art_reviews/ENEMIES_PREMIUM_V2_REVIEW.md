## Review basis

This is the complete regular-enemy category review, not a representative spot check. The exact four-asset CI artifact was compared with the previously committed runtime catalog. All 112 exported frames were decoded and audited, and all nine generated review sheets were opened and inspected: one shared-scale lineup, four native-size full-motion sheets, and four silhouette/material/readability sheets.

The review sheets use the actual runtime PNG pixels without painted cleanup. They expose transparency over checkerboards, preserve native pixels with nearest-neighbor presentation, and include grayscale, silhouette-only, dark-arena, light-arena, attack-impact, and hit-recoil views. The batch remains visually coherent with premium-v2 while giving every enemy a distinct shape language, mass, palette, and combat verb without ornamental clutter.

All art in this category is generated by the repository's offline Blender/Python pipeline. No external texture, model, or generated-image payload was introduced.

### Rootling

The pilot Rootling was retained as the wiry baseline for the category and finalized with stronger carved facial planes, split bark, rooted feet, asymmetric leaf mantle, amber growth-ring focus, and a quicker feint-and-swipe motion language. Its warm bark and restrained green accents stay readable on both arena-value extremes. The revised collapse no longer clips its branch crown.

### Stonekin

The placeholder slab was replaced with an asymmetrical rune bulwark built from interlocking basalt masses, chipped edge planes, broken crown, oversized fists, slab feet, sparse moss, and one controlled cyan core. The broad silhouette communicates durability immediately; restrained idle motion, two-arm anticipation, heavy impact, low-amplitude recoil, and weighty failure distinguish its mass from the other enemies.

### Gloom Wolf

The former rounded two-legged silhouette is now a readable four-legged shadow stalker with a low body wedge, tall ears, deep ruff, facial mask, tiny ivory fangs, articulated paws, and a long swept tail. Near-black violet masses retain a single mid-violet separation plane and cyan eye focus. The stalk, pounce, recoil, and curled collapse frames preserve all extremities and tail inside the atlas.

### Fungal Brute

The former single-cone mushroom is now a layered spore bruiser with a wide cap and gill band, visible stalk face, barrel body, dark roots, heavy fists, belly spores, and asymmetric shoulder buds. Wine red, earthy olive, warm cream, and sparse gold remain grouped into large readable masses. Cap-led anticipation and collapse create a distinct heavy, slightly elastic motion signature without adding screen noise.
